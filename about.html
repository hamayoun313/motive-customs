Our Story

From one truck in a garage to a full-service build shop.

Motive Customs began the way most real shops do — with a personal project that turned into word-of-mouth work, that turned into a full shop. What hasn't changed is the standard: every vehicle that leaves our doors is treated like it's going on our own driveway.

Today we run lighting, body styling, wraps & decals and brake caliper painting under one roof, with our own fabrication, install and detail teams — no outsourcing the parts that matter.
