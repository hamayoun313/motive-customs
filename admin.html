Shop Admin

Edit products, prices, photos and sales. Changes go live on motivecustoms.shop about a minute after you publish.

  1. On GitHub, open Settings → Developer settings → Personal access tokens → Fine-grained tokens, then Generate new token.
  2. Under Repository access, choose Only select repositories → motive-customs.
  3. Under Permissions → Contents, choose Read and write. Create the token and paste it below.

Sale prices

Set a sale price on every product that has a price. Customers see the normal price crossed out next to the sale price, plus a “Sale” badge. You can also change single products from the Products tab.

Sale prices are calculated from your current normal price. Only show a crossed-out price you have genuinely been charging. Raising prices just to “discount” them is misleading under Australian Consumer Law.
